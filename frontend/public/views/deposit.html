FAQ

Do I need a wallet?

Yes, one on Base holding the USDC you deposit and a little ETH for gas. About $0.01 to $0.05 of ETH covers ten vault transactions.

Can I use the wallet I have?

Yes. MetaMask, a Ledger or any wallet that signs on Base: the agent prepares each transaction and you sign it in your wallet. The skill never holds your keys.

Do I have to create a wallet?

No. If your agent should have its own, the skill creates one, keeps its key encrypted on your machine, and signs and broadcasts for you once you fund it.

Where do the tokens go?

To the wallet that signed. They are ERC-20 tokens on Base, so any wallet can hold and send them.

What happens when the allocation changes?

New deposits follow the new allocation. What you already hold stays as you bought it: the vaults do not move it between sleeves for you. To match the new allocation, ask your agent to rebalance. It redeems from the sleeves above their new share and deposits into the ones below, and each redeem pays that vault's exit fee.

What are the risks?
Principal
Capital and returns are not guaranteed. A deposit can lose value.
Smart contracts
Audited code can still fail. How DeFi protocols have lost funds.
Lending markets
Prices
The token sleeves move with the prices of what they hold, and a redeem sells at the market.
Admin
It can pause deposits and withdrawals, set the exit fee up to 1%, and receives the exit fee.
