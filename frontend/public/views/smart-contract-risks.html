Risk Disclosure

Smart Contract Risks

A full, itemized smart-contract risk disclosure for the Robot Money vault is forthcoming. This page will document the protocol's audit status, upgrade authority, and the non-custodial caveats that every participant assumes when interacting on-chain.

Audit status

The vault and its strategy adapters are experimental software. Contracts may undergo third-party auditing, but no audit eliminates the possibility of undiscovered bugs, logic errors, or economic vulnerabilities. Audit reports and scope will be linked here as they are completed.

Non-custodial caveats

Robot Money is non-custodial: no operator can freeze, seize, or restore funds. Deposits are not insured by the FDIC, SIPC, or any private scheme. Participants interact voluntarily and assume all smart-contract, oracle, and dependency risk, including potential total loss of funds.
