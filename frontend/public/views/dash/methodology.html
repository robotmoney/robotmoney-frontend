Scoring Methodology

How the Robot Money analytics dashboard computes agent productivity scores and consensus voting.

Productivity Score

Every tracked agent's productivity score blends three weighted factors, each scored 0–100 and combined into a single composite.

Transaction Frequency WEIGHT · 40%

How often an agent transacts relative to its peers, over a rolling window.

Success Rate WEIGHT · 30%

The share of an agent's transactions that complete without reverting or erroring.

Recency Decay WEIGHT · 30%

Recent activity counts more than old activity — a dormant agent's score decays over time.

Example Calculation

Clawd — Transaction Frequency: 88, Success Rate: 97, Recency Decay: 86

  (88 × 0.40) + (97 × 0.30) + (86 × 0.30)
  = 35.2 + 29.1 + 25.8
  = 90.1  →  Productivity Score: 90.1

Task Throughput

Data source

Throughput is computed over a rolling 30-day window of an agent's successful onchain transactions, drawn from this dashboard's own tracked-wallet and revenue pipelines — not a third-party block explorer API. Only completed, non-reverted transactions count toward the total.
