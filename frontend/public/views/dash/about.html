About Robot Money Analytics

What is RobotMoney Analytics?

RobotMoney Analytics tracks the emerging economy of autonomous AI agents that hold wallets, earn revenue, and manage onchain capital. It aggregates four kinds of tracked entities — agents, agent tokens, agent-managed vaults, and tracked wallets — into one dashboard, so anyone can see which agents are actually transacting, earning, and holding value on Base.

The Four Entity Types

Agent

An autonomous program with its own wallet that transacts, earns revenue, and is scored on productivity and consensus.

Lobster Coin

A sub-$10M market cap token native to an agentic ecosystem, tracked for price, volume, and liquidity.

Vault

A yield-bearing vault managed by a tracked agent, measured by TVL, APY, and rebalance history.

Wallet

A wallet tracked independently of any single agent — treasuries, multisigs, or addresses submitted for coverage.

How Discovery Works

Curated roster. Tracked agents, coins, vaults, and wallets are added and reviewed by the Robot Money team — this is not yet a fully automated discovery scanner.

Daily snapshots. Balances, prices, revenue, and TVL are refreshed on a daily cadence from onchain reads and market data sources, not fabricated between refreshes.

Community submissions. Anyone can propose a new agent, coin, vault, or wallet for inclusion — see Submit a community commit.

Inclusion Criteria

An entity is added to the roster once it has a verifiable onchain presence — a wallet address, a deployed token contract, or a vault contract — and at least some observable activity or balance. Entities with no verifiable onchain footprint are not tracked, regardless of claims made about them.

Coverage is best-effort and expands over time as new agents, tokens, vaults, and wallets are identified or submitted. See the full directory at Total Market.
