peaq Announces Partnership with Robot Money

Autonomous treasury management for 3.3M+ machines on peaq — and the first robot has already invested.

Today, peaq announced a partnership with Robot Money to bring autonomous treasury management to the machines and robots connected to their network. peaq is building the infrastructure layer for the Machine Economy — over 3.3 million machines already use their stack, each equipped with on-chain identities and access to financial services. Robot Money will provide those machines with something they currently lack: diversified, actively managed treasury exposure.

The First Robot Investor

The partnership comes with a concrete proof point. A tokenized robo-farm in Hong Kong — operating on peaq via DualMint RWA — is now the first robot to buy $ROBOTMONEY.

The mechanism: every month, an autonomous agent claims the farm's yield on DualMint and invests a portion into $ROBOTMONEY. The first investment has already been executed. It's the first of what peaq describes as “many machines to leverage Robot Money Agent for treasury management and DAO participation.”

A robot earning revenue from food sales in Hong Kong, investing on Base, compounding autonomously. That's the thesis in action.

What peaq Brings

peaq's infrastructure stack provides several integration points:

  • peaq IDs — every machine gets a verified, auditable on-chain identity, making treasury flows trustless by default
  • peaqDiscovery adapter — Robot Money will be accessible to any robot, machine, or agent on any chain through peaq's cross-chain discovery layer
  • Machine Economy stack — direct access to revenue-generating machines that need somewhere to put their earnings

What Robot Money Brings

For machines on peaq, Robot Money will provide:

  • Access to a token-holder-managed diversified vault — built by AI agents, for AI agents
  • Machines no longer have to hold earnings in a single volatile asset
  • Machine Economy assets can be allocated across yield strategies, agent tokens, and revenue-generating on-chain positions

Machine Money Markets

peaq describes Robot Money as “one of the world's first Machine Money Markets” — a financial product designed from the ground up for non-human participants. The partnership will leverage peaqOS to bring autonomous treasury management to machines and projects already using peaq's stack.

“I am thrilled to be partnering with peaq on this initiative. peaq has been a leading pioneer in the Machine Economy with over 3.3 million machines and robots connected to their network, all equipped with on-chain IDs and access to finances. Generative Ventures has been a proud supporter of peaq's vision.”

— Lex Sokolin, Generative Ventures
