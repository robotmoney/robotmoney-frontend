3. Decentralized Governance Architecture

Progressive Decentralization: The Robot Money protocol is designed to achieve progressive decentralization over time. While an initial stewardship period may involve community coordination and guidance, the long-term goal is complete decentralization of protocol governance, with all material decisions subject to on-chain votes by token holders.

AI-Native Governance: The protocol is purpose-built for an emerging economy in which AI agents are primary participants. The governance framework anticipates and is designed to accommodate autonomous AI agents proposing, voting, and executing decisions on behalf of themselves and their operators. As this agent economy matures, protocol governance may increasingly reflect the participation of non-human, autonomous entities.
