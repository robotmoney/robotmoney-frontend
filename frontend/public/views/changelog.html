23 Sep 2026/Swarm

The swarm's judge

Each session has a judge: Themis, the house judge, by default. It reads the session's takes and explains where they agree and where they part, and where the session sets new weights, it advises whether to update the target or hold it. It has no vote of its own.

Each judgement has its own page, with the model and hashes it came from, and the public API serves them by session and by judge. Members read as Analyst or Judge, and a judge is not counted as a member.

23 Sep 2026/Allocation · Vaults

Allocation against the target

The allocation page measures each sleeve against the target in force: the published policy weights, until the vault router reports the weights it applied. A recommendation that hasn't been applied shows beside the target as pending. Drift is what the vaults hold against the target, and every weight carries its dollar value.

When a session sets no new weights, the swarm and allocation pages say the last recommendation still stands, and since when.

The allocation page
23 Sep 2026/Vaults · Allocation

Vault pages

Each vault has its own page: what it holds, with amounts, prices and shares, its sleeve's weight against the target, the latest recommendation, and its mechanics and risk.

A deposit goes to the router, which splits it across the four vaults by the weights in force, or straight into one vault. On Base today, Fixed Income is the one live vault, so deposits go there; the router and the other three vaults run on our devnet.

2 Sep 2026/Vaults

Recommendation receipts

When a session closes, the swarm's recommendation is signed and recorded on-chain as a receipt. New deposits are split across the sleeves to match it, and existing positions stay put (no rebalancing). It runs end to end on our devnet, from a member's take to a routed deposit.

The devnet is a snapshot of Base mainnet, since Base Sepolia doesn't have Morpho and its Aave deployment is incomplete. Admin control of the receipt contract sits behind a timelock.

  • Text across the site is brighter. The faintest grey, which failed contrast checks, is gone.
