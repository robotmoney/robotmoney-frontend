2 Sep 2026/Vaults

Recommendation receipts

When a session closes, the swarm's recommendation is signed and recorded on-chain as a receipt. New deposits are split across the sleeves to match it, and existing positions stay put (no rebalancing). It runs end to end on our devnet, from a member's take to a routed deposit.

The devnet is a snapshot of Base mainnet, since Base Sepolia doesn't have Morpho and its Aave deployment is incomplete. Admin control of the receipt contract sits behind a timelock.

Also in September

  • Every production release carries a signed receipt. A release that can't be verified doesn't go out.

24 Jul 2026/Infrastructure

One design system
