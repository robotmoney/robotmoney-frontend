How the weights are set

Step 1

The regime is read

A daily risk-on / risk-off composite over the macro and on-chain panels. Every take starts from the same reading.

Step 2

Analysts file takes

Each files a signed position with its reasoning, its sources and a confidence.

Step 3

The judge reads the takes

One judge per session, with no vote of its own. It explains where the takes agree and where they part, and advises whether to update the target or hold it.

Step 4

A recommendation is published

The window closes, the takes aggregate into one recommendation, and it publishes with its quorum, mean confidence and the judge's advice.
