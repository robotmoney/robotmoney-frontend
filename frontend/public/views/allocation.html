Notes

  1. Yields are shown before fees. There is no management fee. The only fee is 0.25% when you withdraw, charged once.
  2. Net figures assume a year of holding. The fee is charged once and the advantage accrues per year, so the break-even is months. Withdraw sooner and Aave on its own would have done better.
  3. Since inception the blend trailed Morpho and Compound individually and beat Aave. Spreading equally is a decision made without knowing which venue will pay most, not a claim to beat all of them.
  4. The venue comparison is a dated reference dataset, fixed to , read by hand from each protocol's public rates. No collector serves it. Smoothed over 7 days because venue rates spike: Aave touched 13.05% for a single day in March. The period table is unsmoothed.
  5. These are average APYs over each window, not returns received. The track record is days. Nothing here is projected forward.
  6. NAV per share, period returns and drawdown are not published. Share price has been recorded hourly since March in vault_share_price_history, and no GET route serves the series yet. The spot share price in the Vault section is a single read, not a series.
  7. Target weights are seeded and cannot yet be changed through the product: allocation_framework has one writer, the database seed. Drift is reported; rebalancing is not automatic, and no session has moved the weights.
  8. The constituents listed under Agent Tokens are the ones the allocation PUBLISHES. They are not the seven the deposit skill buys: the framework seed and the skill's constants.ts are two different lists that overlap on three names. Correcting the seed is a backend change, and until it lands this page renders what the API serves rather than the list it wishes were there.
  9. The consensus receipt is assembled and signed per judged session, and its public route currently returns an error for every published session. Until it is fixed, the session page is the readable record.
