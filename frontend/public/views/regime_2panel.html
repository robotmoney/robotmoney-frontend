Regime Classifier (2-panel reference)

The original two-panel regime classifier — macro and on-chain indicators only, without the later factor panel. This reference view is preserved for comparison and will mirror the live /regime dashboard with the factor panel removed.

Why keep the 2-panel version

The classifier began as a macro + on-chain composite before the factor panel (equity trend, breadth, momentum, valuation) was added. Keeping the original as a reference makes the contribution of the factor panel legible and lets the two labelings be compared side by side over the same history.
