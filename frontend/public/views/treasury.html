FAQ

Robot Money's own money, kept apart from what depositors hold in the vaults. It is three wallets on Base, and every figure here is read from them on chain.

Part of the protocol's swap-fee income is paid in $ROBOTMONEY, and the protocol keeps it. The tokens its buybacks bought stay in the primary wallet too.

Two wallets set up to hand stablecoins to Zyfai and Giza, which manage them for yield through a strategy account each. A wallet's row shows what it holds itself and what its strategy account holds.
