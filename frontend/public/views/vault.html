Allocation

Allocation is the weight each sleeve is meant to carry. Holdings is what it carries today. Drift is the gap between them, and it is closed by where new deposits go, not by selling what the vault already holds.7

Notes

  1. Yields are shown before fees. There is no management fee. The only fee is 0.25% when you withdraw, charged once.
  2. Net figures assume you hold for a year. Because the fee is charged once and the advantage accrues per year, the break-even is months. Withdraw sooner and you would have done better in Aave on its own.
  3. Since inception the vault trailed Morpho and Compound individually, and beat Aave. Spreading equally is a decision made without knowing which venue will pay most, not a claim to beat all of them.
  4. Smoothed over 7 days because venue rates spike: Aave touched 13.05% for a single day in March. The table is unsmoothed.
  5. These are average APYs over each period, not returns you would have received. The vault is days old; nothing here is projected forward.
  6. Drawdown and Sharpe are not published yet. Both need the full history of NAV per share, which has been recorded hourly since March but is not yet served; the current NAV per share above is live.
  7. The vault does not rebalance positions it already holds. New deposits are routed toward the allocation, so drift closes as capital arrives rather than by selling. This is deliberate: rebalancing on a depositor's behalf inside a vault of vaults is expensive and is not something the protocol does. A holder who wants the current allocation can withdraw and re-deposit. Allocation weights are currently seeded and cannot yet be changed through the product.
  8. The venue comparison and the recorded value series are a dated reference dataset, fixed to , not a live feed. Vault TVL, holdings and allocation weights above them are live.
