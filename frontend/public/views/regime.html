Regime Classifier

A daily cross-asset reading of risk-on or risk-off: the arithmetic mean of a macro panel and an on-chain panel. A third panel, equity factor, is tracked beside them for context and left out of the composite. All three recompute every 24 hours from open data, with point-in-time inverse-correlation weights.

Methodology

Composite = 0.5 × macro + 0.5 × on-chain. Each panel is a weighted mean of sign-aligned 3-year rolling percentile ranks; weights within a panel are inversely proportional to each indicator's mean absolute correlation with the rest of the panel. A composite percentile below reads risk-off and above reads risk-on.

How it works

Normalization
A rolling 3-year percentile rank per indicator, sign-aligned so +1 always means risk-on.
Weighting
Point-in-time inverse correlation within each panel. Each day's weights use only the trailing 3-year window, so there is no look-ahead. Redundant inputs are discounted automatically, without manual sub-grouping. No indicator carries more than 25%.
Composite
The arithmetic mean of the macro and on-chain panel indices (the equity factor panel is excluded), ranked as a 3-year rolling percentile and bucketed at 0.33 / 0.67.
Smoothing
The published label switches after 5 consecutive trading days in the new bucket, or on a single-day move in the composite percentile beyond 2σ of its trailing 1-year daily changes, in the new bucket's direction. It filters out flips at a boundary and still follows a fast, real shift.
Storage
The history is kept daily in long format; the current snapshot is served from the analytics store.
