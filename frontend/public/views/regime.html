Regime Classifier

A daily cross-asset reading of risk-on or risk-off: the arithmetic mean of a macro panel and an on-chain panel. A third panel, equity factor, is tracked beside them for context and left out of the composite. All three recompute every 24 hours from open data, with point-in-time inverse-correlation weights.

Methodology

How it works

  • Macro panel (8): 10y–2y curve, real 10y, 5y breakeven, HY OAS, DXY, initial jobless claims, VIX, copper/gold. Sources: FRED and Yahoo.
  • On-chain panel (10): DeFi TVL (level + 90d growth), stablecoin float (level + 90d growth), BTC/ETH active addresses, BTC MVRV, BTC/ETH ratio, ETH 50d/200d trend, new DEX pools per 24h. Sources: DefiLlama, blockchain.com, Coinmetrics, Yahoo, GeckoTerminal.
  • Equity factor panel (8): high-beta vs low-vol (SPHB/SPLV), momentum (MTUM/SPY), growth vs value (IWF/IWD), utilities leadership (XLU/SPY), staples vs discretionary (XLP/XLY), S&P 500 50d/200d trend, IWM/SPY breadth, Shiller CAPE. Sources: Yahoo Finance and a mirror of the Shiller spreadsheet. Sign-stable signals of equity risk appetite, style and valuation, tracked for context and not part of the composite.
  • Normalization: rolling 3-year percentile rank per indicator, sign-aligned so +1 always means risk-on.
  • Within-panel weighting: point-in-time inverse-correlation. Each day's weights use only the trailing 3-year window, so there is no look-ahead. Redundant inputs are discounted automatically, without manual sub-grouping. 25% per-indicator cap.
  • Composite: the arithmetic mean of the macro and on-chain panel indices (the equity factor panel is excluded), ranked as a 3-year rolling percentile and bucketed at 0.33 / 0.67.
  • Smoothing: the published regime label requires 5 consecutive trading days in the new bucket before switching, OR a single-day move in composite percentile exceeding 2σ of its trailing 1y daily-change distribution (with direction consistent with the new bucket). Filters out boundary-noise flips while still responding quickly to legitimate fast regime shifts.
  • Storage: the history is kept daily in long format; the current snapshot is served from the analytics store.

Two panels, plus a third for context

The macro panel reads upstream causes: monetary policy, credit conditions and the labor market. The on-chain panel reads crypto-native flows and network health. These two independent viewpoints are averaged into the composite. The equity factor panel reads the behavior of price-setters who have already digested the upstream inputs and are committing capital; it is tracked beside the composite and not blended into it.
