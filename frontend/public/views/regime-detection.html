Research Brief

Regime Detection

A survey of prior art in market-regime detection and a mapping of the design space that informs the Robot Money classifier. The full brief is being written; this page will host the literature review and the rationale behind the composite, panel-based approach.

Prior-art survey

Regime models span Markov-switching estimators, volatility-target rules, trend and breadth composites, and cross-asset risk indicators. This brief will catalog the major families, their assumptions, and where each breaks down — the groundwork for a classifier built to be transparent and reproducible rather than opaque.

Design-space mapping

The Robot Money classifier reads indicators across independent macro, on-chain, and factor panels and produces a daily composite label. See the live classification and current methodology at robotmoney.net/regime.
