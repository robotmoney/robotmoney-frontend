Robot Money · home hero · concepts · 8 Oct 2026

Many minds, one allocation, held safely.

Five directions for the hero. Each one has to say three things at a glance: this is frontier AI, many models reach one answer together, and money is safe here. Concepts 1 to 4 leave the current palette behind; concept 5 takes the current design direction, Lex's, to its most premium form. The images were made with Codex image generation on the ChatGPT subscription. Each concept notes how the hero would move.

Concept 1

Guilloche

The idea
Banknotes and bonds use guilloche engraving because it cannot be faked. Here every agent draws one line, and together the lines weave a single secure rosette: the allocation.
How the hero moves
Each swarm member's take is drawn live as its own guilloche curve, in its own ink. As takes come in, the curves interlock into one rosette whose four lobes are sized by the published weights. A deposit is printed into it as a fine new line. Vector line art in WebGL, razor sharp at any size.
Why it earns trust
It borrows the visual language of money itself, on ivory paper: a calm, light register no crypto site uses.
Risk
Light theme is a big change from today's dark site.

Concept 2 Built

Prism

The idea
Every model is a beam of its own colour. The crystal combines them into one clean beam: collective intelligence as optics.
How the hero moves
A real-time refraction shader. Each member enters as a beam in its own hue; when a session closes, the beams converge and the white beam leaves the crystal and splits into four channels sized by the weights, carrying deposits to the vaults.
Why it earns trust
Precise, scientific, expensive: an instrument, not a casino.
Risk
Rainbow beams can read as generic "AI art" if not handled with restraint.

Concept 3 Built

Murmuration

The idea
Swarm intelligence as nature does it: thousands of independent birds, one coherent shape, no leader.
How the hero moves
A live flocking simulation of thousands of agents over the photographed sky. They wander, then settle into a ring whose arcs are the allocation's weights, then stream down to the four vaults on the horizon.
Why it earns trust
Calm and human; it makes "many agents" feel natural rather than threatening.
Risk
Less obviously about money; the vaults and the weights must carry that.

Concept 4

Glass vessel

The idea
Money held in the open: one deposit divided exactly between four chambers you can see into.
How the hero moves
A 3D glass vessel with real refraction. Deposits fall as beads; a divider set by the swarm's weights routes them, and each chamber fills to its real target. Hovering a chamber names its sleeve and vault.
Why it earns trust
Transparency made literal; tactile and premium, like a luxury product shot.
Risk
The swarm itself is not visible; it needs a second element to show the agents.

Concept 5 New

The Wedge Monument

The idea
The Robot Money mark, a circle pierced by a wedge, built as a monument: a black stone disc as tall as a tower on an empty salt flat, with a wedge cut clean through it and the light of the swarm pouring out. It is Lex's direction at full scale: the treasury of machine money, Lissitzky's wedge, one cyan light source in the dark.
How the hero moves: render and live overlay
The render is the plate. Each member's take arrives out of the night sky as a cyan line and converges on the wedge; as the session closes, the light through the cut builds. The published allocation is the wedge of light on the ground, which reaches toward the viewer and divides into lanes, one per asset in the mix. Deposits travel up the lanes into the monument as points of light, and green fills the base as the money arrives.
How the hero moves: real-time 3D
The same monument as live geometry: a stone disc, a cut and a volumetric shaft of light rendered in WebGL. The camera drifts round the monument and the light falls across a moving ground; the swarm gathers as particles of light inside the cut. No photograph, so it stays sharp at any size and every element can follow the data.
Why it earns trust
It is the mark itself, carried at monumental scale: permanent, heavy, institutional. Of the five, it is the only one that could only be Robot Money.
Risk
Monument renders can read as a film poster if the story is not legible; the lines, the lanes and the money have to carry it. The light bends the covenant (haze, bloom on the beam) by choice.
