**Regime read, November 20, 2026 (model v3, live API): risk-off, composite 0.32**

Stress has spread from stocks to credit and crypto. Expected stock volatility, junk-bond spreads and the real
interest rate are all near the top of their 3-year ranges, and money is leaving crypto through stablecoins. The job
market is still strong.

Production's composite averages the macro (0.32) and on-chain (0.33) panels; the equity factor panel (0.35) is shown
for context only. At 0.32 the composite is the lowest reading in the 400 days this data covers. It was 0.47 thirty
days ago and 0.56 ninety days ago.

**Rates, credit and volatility (macro): the main source of pressure**
- VIX 28.23, model reading 3rd: investors expect large stock swings. The S&P 500 and the Dow entered a correction (a
  fall of 10% or more from a peak) on November 19 in a selloff led by AI stocks [1].
- High-yield credit spread 3.52%, 8th: lenders want more extra yield on junk bonds over Treasuries, a sign of
  caution.
- Real 10-year yield 2.88%, 2nd: money is expensive after inflation. Minutes of the Fed's October meeting, released
  November 18, showed almost all officials expect further rate hikes [2].
- Holding it up: initial jobless claims 203,000, 87th. Few people are filing for unemployment.

**Crypto (on-chain): weak demand and money leaving**
- BTC MVRV 1.16, 1st: Bitcoin's price is close to what holders paid on average. Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on
  November 20 for the first time since July, falling together with stocks [3].
- Stablecoin supply −5.1% over 90 days, 1st: money is leaving crypto. Glassnode measured a $14.2 billion drop in
  stablecoin supply over the 30 days to November 9, the fastest since June 2022 [4].
- BTC active addresses 487,454, 33rd: Bitcoin network use is below the middle of its range.

**US stocks (equity factor, context only): out of growth**
- Growth vs value 0th and momentum 10th: growth stocks and recent winners have lost the most, in line with a selloff
  centered on AI and big tech stocks [1].
- S&P 500 50-day/200-day trend 1.006, 14th: the uptrend is close to flat.

**What it adds up to:** the pressure is in financial markets. Higher expected rates, wider credit spreads and a stock
selloff are pulling down risk appetite, and crypto is falling with stocks rather than apart from them. The job market
is the main offset. The composite has gone from 0.56 ninety days ago to 0.32, its lowest reading in this data.
